The Story Behind This

"When I first came to IIT Jodhpur in my first year, finding past papers, tutorials, and lecture slides in one organized place was scattered and frustrating. I didn't want my juniors to feel disheartened or struggle before exams. That's why I created this archive — and I'll continue maintaining it till my final year so this legacy stays alive."

— Shreshth Dhimole (B25, IIT Jodhpur)

Academic Framework

Evaluation Structure & Tips

20% - 30%
Continuous Assessment
Quizzes, surprise tests, weekly problem sets, and tutorial attendance.
25% - 30%
Mid-Semester (Minor)
Comprehensive test covering the first half of the syllabus.
40% - 50%
End-Semester (Major)
Final cumulative examination covering the entire course syllabus.
